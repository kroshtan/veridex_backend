# Veridex

Veridex analyses e-commerce product pages for legitimacy using a parallel pipeline of skills: **page_content** performs a broad LLM scan of the cleaned listing text for scam signals, dropship copy, and missing trust markers; **reverse_image_search** reverse-searches product images via Google to detect known dropship source domains such as AliExpress or Temu; **reddit_brand** extracts the seller name and searches Reddit for fraud reports or community sentiment; **exif_check** downloads product images and inspects their EXIF metadata for discrepancies with listing claims, such as photo dates predating the product or GPS coordinates inconsistent with the seller's claimed location; **html_source_signals** scans the raw HTML source for hard-coded dropship app identifiers (Oberlo, DSers, CJ Dropshipping, etc.) and AliExpress CDN links, then analyses the page's technical fingerprint for softer signals; **domain_age** performs a WHOIS lookup on the page domain and flags recently registered, short-lived, or privacy-hidden domains typical of pop-up stores; **review_integrity** extracts product reviews from JSON-LD and microdata (following review links where needed) and analyses rating distributions, date patterns, and linguistic signals for fake or manipulated reviews; and **claims_verification** extracts explicit quality, performance, and certification claims from the listing and assesses each one for physical plausibility, price–quality coherence, and certification authenticity.
